Your neighborhood coffeehouse

The kind of place that feels familiar.

Since 1996, Bru House has been part of the rhythm of New Brighton—morning coffee before work, breakfast with family, lunch with a friend, and a comfortable table when you need a moment to slow down.

We are independently family-owned, and we believe the small things matter: a warm welcome, a thoughtfully made drink, and food that makes your day a little better.
